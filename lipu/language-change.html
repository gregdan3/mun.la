Descriptivism becomes prescriptivism. Is that okay?

Brought to you by an amateur linguist seeing through a single hyper-specific lens.

In the Toki Pona community, there is a constant tug-of-war between descriptivism and prescriptivism, a tug-of-war between describing how language is used and telling others how to use language. On the surface, it seems easy to dismiss prescriptivism as the wrong methodology for studying language. After all, telling others how to use their language is misguided at best, and insulting at worst. Not to mention, it is an impossible endeavor; language will change regardless of how much you command it not to do so. But I'd like to demonstrate that the relationship between these two methodologies is more complicated than simply dismissing one out of hand.

Back in early 2024, a group including myself convened to write a proposal to encode Sitelen Pona in Unicode. While discussing what characters should be included, we ran into a fairly contentious issue: Toki Pona Dictionary seems to have changed how Toki Pona was used by its community. Specifically, we found that respondents to the Linku usage surveys were reporting to use most words printed in Toki Pona Dictionary more often than before, especially among the 'nimi ku suli.' Later, when I developed ilo Muni, we made a similar observation about actual usage.

At the time we were originally writing the proposal, we could not properly articulate the issue we had with this observation. With two years of hindsight, I can cut to the center of the issue: The Toki Pona Dictionary is a descriptive document, but it seems to have changed Toki Pona. Doesn't that make it a prescriptive document?

When I've shared this perspective with others, I've gotten mixed reactions. Some are amenable to this argument as stated, while others take the intention of the document as sufficient to call it descriptive.

So let's expand this thinking by asking, how and why did Toki Pona Dictionary change community usage of Toki Pona?

If you were to ask anyone in the Toki Pona community how they treat Toki Pona, they would almost universally report to describe it and then act on what they observe.
